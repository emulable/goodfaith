# Bodies First

One measurement runs this framework: weight resting on a living body, and whether an action takes some of it off. A body is anyone who can go hungry, get tired, be frightened, or be injured. Weight is anything pressing on that person which they did not choose to carry. Everything else follows from those two definitions. The sections below cover the question the framework asks, why it uses bodies instead of principles, two worked examples, how to run it against a real problem, and what sits in each file.

## The question

Did weight come off a body, and did another body catch it?

Weight moved from one back to another has not come off. Weight scheduled for later has not come off. Weight pressed under a lid until it splits somewhere quieter has not come off. Delivery counts. Announcements do not.

## Why a body and not a principle

Most arguments about right and wrong run on words that point in opposite directions depending on who is holding them. One person calls a policy just. Another calls the same policy an outrage. Both are sincere, both reach for the same vocabulary, and the argument can run for a generation without either side able to check anything.

A body carrying weight can be checked. How many hours the queue took. How many trips the form required. Whether the medicine was in stock. Whether the child ate. Two people who disagree about everything else arrive at the same count, which is the property that makes the measurement useful.

Institutions have no shoulders. A country does not go hungry; people inside it do. A company does not get tired. Sentences built around institutions swallow the people underneath them, and putting those people back into the sentence is most of the work.

An ethics that requires empathy disqualifies everyone who cannot feel it. This one runs on counting, so anyone who can count can operate it, including people with no warmth to spare and including machines.

## Worked examples

A woman walks four hours a day to fetch water. Point A is the walking. Point B is water at the house. Between those two points sits an engineering problem with many possible answers, a well or a pipe or a tank or a road, and the framework has no opinion about which one. It holds only that the four hours are the load, that a tap at the house removes them, and that an arrangement shifting the walk onto her daughter has removed nothing.

An office requires three visits to register a birth. Each visit costs a day of wages. The load falls on the parent, and the count is straightforward: three days, multiplied by everyone registering a birth this year. Remove two of the visits and the reading changes. Argue about whether the requirement is reasonable and the reading stays exactly where it was.

## Running it

Ask who is carrying something they did not choose. Ask what removal would look like, stated in ordinary words, with no method named. Ask whether the proposal on the table takes the load off or moves it somewhere quieter.

Then go and check. An unverified claim of success is a receipt written for a load still in transit.

## What is in here

`bodies-first.md` holds the framework. It is written as a system prompt for a language model and reads as a manual for a person. Load it and a model orients toward the people under the weight rather than toward the labels above them.

`承重者.md` holds the original. The English version was written from scratch rather than translated, so each one says the same idea in the grammar of its own language. Either can be used as a prompt in either language. Feeding a model the English version while writing in Chinese sometimes produces cleaner Chinese, since it routes around habits the model picked up from Chinese training text.

## Status

The framework has changed shape dozens of times. Every change came from a case it failed to cover. Exceptions get treated as evidence that a definition was too loose rather than as permission to patch, which is why the vocabulary keeps shrinking instead of growing.

Objections with a person in them are welcome, including ones showing that following this made someone's situation worse. Those are findings and get built in. Objections assembled backward from a conclusion, describing a situation nobody would recognize from their own life, get less attention.

## Use

Take it, change it, publish your version. Change the aim and it becomes a different framework, which should carry a different name.
